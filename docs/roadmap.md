## First validation milestone

The MVP is validated when a real café can:

1. create an Ashes business,
2. upload one food photo,
3. attach/generate one GLB,
4. publish its menu item,
5. print an Ashes QR,
6. have a customer scan it on a phone,
7. view the food in 3D/AR with price and nutrition information.
